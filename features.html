How ReportHub works

Each role gets tools designed for its responsibilities while working from the same school data.

👑 School Owner / Owner Dashboard

Central oversight and control across schools using the platform.

  • Monitor registered schools and school activity
  • Control school access and account status
  • Manage school administrators and teacher accounts
  • Monitor marks and academic activity
  • Review system activity and audit records
  • Check system health and operational status
  • Control individual-school settings
  • Support password and account controls
  • Manage academic/system-wide settings
  • View important platform information for supervision

🏫 School Administrator

Runs the school's daily academic and administrative setup.

  • Enroll and manage students
  • Capture parent/guardian name, phone and email
  • Organize classes and student records
  • Manage subjects and paper setup
  • Add or delete assessment papers where required
  • Manage teachers and school users
  • Set academic information for reporting
  • Promote students or repeat a class when required
  • Send emails to all, teachers or parents
  • Prepare the school for report generation

👨‍🏫 Teacher Dashboard

Simple tools for teachers to record and manage academic results.

  • Access assigned classes and subjects
  • Enter Midterm marks
  • Enter End-of-Term marks
  • Record marks according to configured papers
  • Save student results securely
  • View totals and grades
  • Add teacher comments where applicable
  • Work with the current academic term/year
  • Update account password
  • Support accurate printable school reports
